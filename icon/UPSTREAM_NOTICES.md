# Third-Party Assets And Notices

English translation of the upstream source inventory previously retained here. Original statements and full upstream license files remain available from [aaANDkk/Qure_Style_SVG](https://github.com/aaANDkk/Qure_Style_SVG); the inventory also identifies [aaANDkk/ClashConfig](https://github.com/aaANDkk/ClashConfig) as a source of finished SVGs and overview images. Original PNG reference material is not distributed here.

This inventory describes the broader upstream collection, not a claim that every listed asset is shipped in this repository. Each asset retains its original license; there is no single license for the entire icon set. The locally retained MIT geometry notices are in [geometry-and-qure.txt](geometry-and-qure.txt).
